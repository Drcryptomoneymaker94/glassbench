**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: The bull case is real: NVDA posted ~106% YoY revenue growth, ~75% gross margins, a powerful CUDA/full-stack moat, and an optically cheap 14.4x forward multiple. But the bear won the risk/reward argument on the quality and durability of that growth. The forward EPS used to make the stock look cheap assumes earnings roughly double in the next year, while reported EPS is flattered by non-operating gains; normalized Q2 EPS was closer to $2.19 than $2.46, and Q1 was similarly inflated. Meanwhile cash conversion deteriorated sharply: OCF was only 40% of net income, receivables grew +55% QoQ versus +17.9% revenue growth, and DSO jumped from ~45 to ~60 days. The balance sheet is also changing character, with restricted cash surging to $36.9B and total debt rising from $11B to $38.4B. The macro backdrop is hostile for a high-beta, long-duration growth stock: 10Y at 5.22%, 76% odds of another Fed hike, and sticky inflation. Technicals have rolled over below the 10 EMA/VWMA, RSI has no oversold cushion, and insiders are selling into strength. The bull's rebuttals—buybacks, deferred revenue, and the idea that rates do not matter for a net-cash company—do not fully offset the cash-conversion and financing red flags. NVDA remains a great company, but the near-term evidence supports trimming exposure rather than buying this dip.

**Strategic Actions**: Set NVDA to an Underweight position versus a standard allocation. For a model portfolio, trim to roughly 0.70-0.75x the normal weight rather than exiting entirely. Execute sales into strength, not at the lows: use the 231.5-231.7 reclaim zone as the first supply area, and if the bounce extends, use 237-243 as a more favorable trim zone. Do not add new long exposure at current price. Treat 221.94 (50-day SMA) as the key line in the sand: a decisive close below it opens the path toward the lower Bollinger band near 210.64. Avoid the bull's overly tight stop near 220.80; instead use a wider, volatility-based risk level or a close-below-221.94 confirmation to avoid noise-driven whipsaw. Hold any remaining position only if cash-flow quality improves, specifically DSO stabilizes/declines, OCF conversion recovers from ~40%, and the rate/CPI setup stops deteriorating. Rebuild toward neutral only after those signals appear.
