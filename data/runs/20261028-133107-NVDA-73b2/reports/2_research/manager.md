The bull case wins on fundamentals: a PEG of 0.26 against ~100% expected earnings growth is genuinely hard to argue with, and the balance sheet/margin profile means NVDA can absorb a multiple compression without breaking. The deferred-revenue build (current liabilities) is a leading indicator of locked-in future demand, not a liability. On the macro point, the bear's strongest argument—that 10Y >5% and zero Fed cuts compress the multiple—is real but partially already reflected: the stock has pulled back from $239 to ~$218 precisely because the market is repricing that risk. The bear is asking us to sell into a pullback that has already discounted the rate headwind, while the bull is buying at a level where the worst of the multiple compression may be behind us.

The bear's strongest points are competitive erosion (OpenAI sourcing Google TPUs, Cerebras on Oracle) and the technical weakness below the 50-day SMA with low volume. These are legitimate reasons to not go all-in at $218—the moat is showing its first cracks, and there is no volume confirmation of accumulation yet. Net: the fundamental value case is decisively stronger than the bear's macro/technical caution, but the bear correctly identifies real execution risk that argues against maximum conviction. This lands on Overweight—constructive, grow exposure—but not a full Buy, because the rate environment and early competitive signals mean we should scale in rather than commit at once.
