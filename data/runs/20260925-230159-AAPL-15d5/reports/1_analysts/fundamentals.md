## 6. Risk Assessment

1. **Valuation risk (highest):** 39x TTM / 35.6x forward with PEG 2.7 leaves little margin for error. Any growth deceleration compresses the multiple sharply.
2. **Inventory build:** +87% YoY — watch for either demand confirmation or margin-damaging markdowns.
3. **Working capital / current ratio at 1.00:** limited short-term cushion (structural, but notable).
4. **Concentration & regulation:** iPhone concentration and ongoing Services/App Store antitrust and regulatory pressures globally.
5. **China / tariff exposure:** supply chain and demand sensitivity remain material.
6. **Insider selling overhang:** persistent executive sales offer no insider-confirmed floor.
