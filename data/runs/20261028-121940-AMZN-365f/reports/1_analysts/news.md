## 4. Synthesis: What This Means for Trading AMZN

### Bull Case
1. **Consumer resilience:** Unemployment at 4.2% supports retail sales — AMZN's core business benefits directly.
2. **Recession risk is minimal (7%):** In a soft-landing scenario, e-commerce and cloud spending remain healthy.
3. **Zoox commercialization progress:** Long-term optionality on autonomous delivery adds value.
4. **AWS AI monetization:** If AMZN can demonstrate accelerating AI-driven AWS growth in Q3 earnings, the stock could re-rate higher.

### Bear Case
1. **ETF selling pressure:** Passive fund outflows create mechanical headwinds near-term.
2. **Sticky inflation + rising rates:** CPI +1.05% over two months and Fed funds at 3.75% with potential for further tightening could compress consumer spending and corporate IT budgets.
3. **AI spending scrutiny:** If AMZN cannot justify its massive AI CapEx spend with commensurate revenue growth, multiple compression is likely.
4. **Yield curve flattening:** From +45 bps to +29 bps — a warning sign that growth may be decelerating ahead.

### Neutral/Catalyst View
- **Q3 earnings are the inflection point.** The next 1–2 weeks will determine whether AMZN is viewed as an AI/cloud growth story or a CapEx-heavy value destroyer.
- **Fed meeting this week** — any hawkish surprise could pressure tech valuations broadly; dovish surprise could provide a tailwind.
