## 1. Company Profile and Overview

Amazon.com, Inc. is a global technology and e-commerce giant operating across multiple business segments including online retail, cloud computing (AWS), digital streaming, advertising, and artificial intelligence services. As of October 28, 2026, AMZN has a market capitalization of approximately **$2.73 trillion**, making it one of the most valuable companies in the world.

The company trades at a **P/E ratio (TTM) of 20.36** and has a **Forward PE of 24.26**, suggesting moderate valuation relative to its growth trajectory. The PEG ratio of **1.44** indicates the stock is reasonably priced relative to expected earnings growth, though slightly above the ideal 1.0 threshold.
