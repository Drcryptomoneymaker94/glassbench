Still, at ~21.8x forward earnings for a ~32% forward EPS grower with elite margins and a widening AI/cloud footprint, the long-term risk/reward is constructive enough to justify gradual accumulation rather than avoidance. The decisive issue is that the bear case is mostly a cash-conversion and multiple-compression story, while the bull case is anchored in accelerating core growth and durable competitive position.
