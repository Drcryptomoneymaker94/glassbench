## 7. Main Risks to the Bull Case

### Risk 1: AI Capex Cycle Slowdown (Probability: Medium-High)
If hyperscalers (Microsoft, Meta, Google, Amazon) reduce data center capital expenditure due to ROI concerns on AI investments, NVDA's revenue growth could decelerate sharply from 65%+ to single digits. This would compress the multiple from 40x+ toward 25–30x, implying a target of $150–$180. **This is the single largest threat to the bull thesis.**

### Risk 2: Competitive Erosion (Probability: Medium)
AMD's MI300/MI400 series, custom ASICs from Google (TPU), Amazon (Trainium), and Microsoft (Maia) are gaining traction. If NVDA's data center market share declines from ~85% to below 70%, pricing power erodes and gross margins compress from 71% toward 60%. This would reduce both EPS growth and multiple expansion potential.

### Risk 3: Regulatory/Geopolitical Headwinds (Probability: Medium)
US export restrictions on advanced AI chips to China could eliminate 15–20% of data center revenue. Further escalation of trade tensions or antitrust scrutiny of NVDA's dominant position could impose structural constraints on growth. A China revenue cliff would require ~$30B+ in new demand from other regions to offset — a non-trivial challenge.
