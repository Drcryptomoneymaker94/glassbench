## Price action and technicals

**A correction inside an intact, but twelve-month-long, consolidation.**

From the 6-October record close of 239.24 the stock fell to 210.96 on 23 October — a drawdown of 11.8% — then recovered 3.3% over two sessions to 217.94. The decline was orderly: 239.24 → 237.47 → 230.48 → 229.28 → 226.32, with the sharp leg on 22–23 October (212.21, then 210.96). The 15–20 October shelf (217.23 / 214.13 / 217.09 / 216.47) is roughly where price has returned, so this is a **double-bottom at 211–214, not yet a confirmed higher low.**

The wider picture matters more than the October wiggle. Over twelve months the stock is up just 8.7% (200.55 → 217.94) and has chopped between 169.14 and 243.37, while the business more than doubled revenue (see below). That is what a stock looks like when it de-rates from a scarcity premium toward a market multiple while fundamentals compound — the compression is in the multiple, not the earnings.

The constructive tell is volume. The 22–23 October sell-off ran on 125.4M and 80.2M shares; the 26–27 October rebound ran on 90.8M and 84.5M. Selling into the dip was not escalating into distribution, and the recovery came on decent participation.

Map: resistance 227.2 (late-September shelf) and then the 239–243 record zone. Support 210.2–211.0 (October low, tested twice), with the 200 round figure beneath as the line that would break the year-long uptrend structure.

## Fundamentals and latest statements

From NVIDIA's Q2 FY27 release covering the quarter ended 2026-07-26:

- Revenue **$96.2B**, up **18%** sequentially and **106%** year over year.
- Gross margin **75.0%** on both a GAAP and non-GAAP basis, versus **72.4%** a year earlier — margin *expanding* at scale, which is the single most important line in the print: mix is moving to full rack-scale systems (GPU, Spectrum-X networking, software), not just more dies.
- FY27 Q3 guidance: revenue **$108B ±(omitted※)** at roughly **74%** gross margin, **explicitly assuming zero Data Center compute revenue from China.**
- GAAP diluted EPS came in *above* non-GAAP EPS in the quarter, meaning GAAP is being flattered by non-operating income — a quality caveat, not a growth caveat.
- Balance sheet remains effectively unconstrained: no leverage barrier to the capital-return program.

Capital allocation: the board added **$150B** to the buyback authorization on 2026-09-28, leaving roughly **$235B** available, and NVIDIA agreed on 2026-09-03 to acquire **Hugging Face for about $12.93B**, expected to close in H1 2027. Recent Form 4 and Form 144 filings show routine option-related insider flow; no cluster of large discretionary sales appears in the last 60 days.

## Valuation

The clean way to frame it with the evidence at hand: **the stock is up 8.7% over twelve months while revenue is up 106% year over year.** Whatever the multiple was a year ago, it has roughly halved relative to the earnings base while estimates kept rising — consensus FY28 EPS saw seven upward revisions against none downward in the last 30 days. So the market is no longer paying a scarcity premium for the growth rate; it is paying something close to a market multiple for triple the market's growth.

That is the core of the long case, and it explains the frustrating tape: good news has stopped moving the stock because the multiple has already compressed and the debate has migrated elsewhere — to **whether customers can finance and power the build-out.**
