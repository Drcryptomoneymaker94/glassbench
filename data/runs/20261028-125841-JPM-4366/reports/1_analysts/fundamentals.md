## **1. Company Profile**

**JPMorgan Chase & Co. (JPM)** is the largest bank in the United States and one of the most diversified financial services firms globally. Headquartered in New York City, JPM operates across four primary business segments:
- **Consumer & Community Banking (CCB):** Retail banking, credit cards, home lending, and small business services
- **Corporate & Investment Bank (CIB):** Investment banking, market-making, securities clearing, and treasury services
- **Commercial Banking (CB):** Lending and financial solutions for mid-market companies
- **Asset & Wealth Management (AWM):** Investment management and wealth advisory services
