Critically, the bull's own base case—25x forward EPS of $3.53—produces ~$88, essentially today's price, meaning upside depends on multiple expansion to 27-30x for a staples company already trading near 25x forward with a 2.4% yield and a PEG of roughly 3-4 on sustainable growth. The market is not ignoring the inflection; it is already paying a premium. The cash-flow argument also cut against the bull: FCF is volatile, the ~$14.3B trailing figure conveniently excludes the large Q1'25 outflow, and the 166% FY25 FCF payout remains a warning. Insiders selling into strength with zero 2026 open-market buys and the stock sitting within 5% of its 52-week high further weaken the risk/reward. That tilts the call to Underweight—but not Sell—because the franchise quality, 61-year dividend growth, improving balance sheet, and normalizing cash generation argue for keeping a core position.

Do not add at current ~$88 levels. Keep a core position rather than exiting. Rebuild only on better risk/reward: start reassessing around $79-82 (the 50-DMA/200-DMA zone) and add more only near $70-75 (~20x forward EPS) or if evidence emerges of volume-led growth and sustained FCF coverage. Revisit the rating after H2'26 data confirms whether operating income growth and cash-flow normalization support the bull's reacceleration thesis.
