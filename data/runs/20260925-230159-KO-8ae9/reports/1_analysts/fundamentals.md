## 8. Key Risks

1. **Valuation risk (primary):** 26.4x TTM / 24.9x forward with PEG 4.0 and a 2.41% yield below historical norms. Multiple compression is the main downside driver if growth disappoints.
2. **Working-capital / cash-conversion volatility:** FY24–FY25 OCF was severely depressed by the IRS deposit and payables swings. While FY26 is recovering, this undermines the reliability of "dividend covered by FCF" narratives.
3. **FX headwinds:** ~$12.3B cumulative FX translation loss; a strong dollar is a persistent drag on reported revenue/earnings (roughly half of revenue is non-US).
4. **Ongoing IRS transfer-pricing litigation** — the source of the ~$6.5B 2024 deposit; ultimate resolution remains an overhang.
5. **Volume/health trends:** GLP-1 adoption, sugar taxes, and health-conscious consumer shifts pose structural risk to carbonated soft drink volumes; growth is currently price/mix-led rather than volume-led.
6. **Leverage:** Despite improvement, gross debt of $43.5B and D/E of 1.16x leave limited flexibility if rates rise or cash flow disappoints.
7. **Lumpy equity income:** Earnings from equity interests ($384–604M/quarter) are volatile and tied to bottling partners.
8. **Insider selling at highs** as detailed above.

## 9. Key Positives / Catalysts

1. **Revenue acceleration:** Q2'26 +6.7% YoY — far above the ~2% historical norm.
2. **EPS growth:** Q2'26 +17% YoY; forward EPS $3.53 implies ~16% growth over FY25's $3.04.
3. **Margin expansion:** Gross margin 61.9% (from 58.1% in FY22); operating margin ~34.9%.
4. **Deleveraging:** Net debt -$9.2B YoY (-23%); net debt/EBITDA ~1.8x.
5. **Balance-sheet repair:** Tangible book value swung from -$6.6B to +$8.2B; equity +26.5% YoY.
6. **Cash flow inflecting hard:** H1'26 OCF already exceeds full-year FY2025; Q2'26 FCF +51% YoY.
7. **Portfolio pruning:** $3.57B of business sales in FY25, $5.34B assets held for sale — sharpening focus on higher-margin concentrate.
8. **Defensive characteristics:** Beta 0.34, 61-year dividend growth streak (Dividend King), ~2.4% yield.
9. **Technical momentum:** Trading above 50-DMA and 200-DMA, within 5% of 52-week high.
