This is the single largest risk — if the "AI gold rush" slows, NVDA's multiple would compress even if earnings remain strong. **Invalidation threshold**: Two consecutive quarters of <20% revenue growth with downward guidance.

### Risk 2: Competitive Threat from Custom Chips (Probability: Medium)
Google (TPU), Amazon (Trainium/Inferentia), Microsoft ( Maia), and Meta are all developing custom AI accelerators that could displace NVDA GPUs for specific workloads. While NVDA's CUDA moat is formidable, sustained customer investment in alternatives could erode market share over time. **Invalidation threshold**: A major hyperscaler announces a multi-billion dollar shift away from NVDA to custom silicon with comparable performance.

### Risk 3: Geopolitical/Export Restriction Escalation (Probability: Medium)
Further U.S. export restrictions on advanced AI chips to China and other regions could eliminate 15-20% of NVDA's addressable market. The company has already adapted by creating "downgraded" chips for China, but further restrictions could limit even those options. **Invalidation threshold**: Complete ban on sales to China with no workaround products permitted.

### Risk 4: Valuation Mean Reversion (Probability: Low-Medium)
Even at 22x forward earnings, NVDA remains expensive by historical standards. A broader market correction or sector rotation away from technology could compress the multiple regardless of fundamentals. **Invalidation threshold**: S&P 500 enters a bear market with NVDA falling >30% on multiple compression alone.
